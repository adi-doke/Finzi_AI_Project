About FinZi

FinZi is a finance companion for people who want one clean place to see where their money is going, upload receipts, and read monthly summaries without jumping across tools.

  • Sign up in a few steps and start with a secure personal account.
  • Log in to view your dashboard, chat with the assistant, and review your money data.
  • Keep your records organized with receipt uploads and monthly reporting tools.
